About This Project

What This Tool Does

This tool uses AI to watch a video of a child interacting with a caregiver and gives you a friendly summary of what it observes. It looks at four key areas:

Movement & Motor Skills

Is your child reaching, crawling, grasping, walking, or hitting other movement milestones?

Caregiver Engagement

How is the adult interacting? Are they talking, holding, playing, or encouraging the child?

Safety Awareness

Are there any visible safety concerns in the environment or interaction?

Learning Support

Is the caregiver helping the child learn something new, like guiding them through a task?

This tool provides observations only — it does not diagnose or replace professional guidance. Always consult a pediatrician for developmental concerns.
